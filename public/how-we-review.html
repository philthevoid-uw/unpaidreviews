How we review

Trust is the only thing this site offers, so our rules are written down and apply to every review.

Who writes the reviews

Reviews are written by our community of reviewers, not by the site's owners or a single critic. Before anyone can post, we screen them:

  • Real people. We verify that each reviewer is a real person and not a bot, and we do not allow automated or AI-written reviews.
  • Not employees. Reviewers cannot be employees, owners or contractors of the business, brand or place they review, or of its competitors.
  • Not funded to review. Reviewers cannot be paid, sponsored or otherwise funded by anyone to provide a review. Reviewers who break these rules are removed and their reviews taken down.
  • Disclosed. Each review shows how the reviewer obtained the item or experience.

Independence rules

  • No payments that influence a review. We do not accept payments, gifts or perks that could shape what a review says or how it scores.
  • No affiliate income. We are not paid for affiliate links, commissions or referral fees, and we do not try to influence the site or anyone's purchase decisions in other ways.
  • No revenue incentive over coverage. No revenue incentive influences what is reviewed or not reviewed, or whether a review is positive or negative.
  • Advertising is visible. Any advertising or revenue-generating promotion is clearly labeled, and we are not influenced by who does or does not advertise here.
  • Disclosure on every review. Each page states how the item was obtained, including anything that was provided, discounted or hosted.
  • Community reviews follow the same policy. Every review is written by our community, so every review must follow these rules, and the community is built on these guiding principles.
  • No conflicts of interest. A reviewer with any relationship to what they review does not review it, or discloses the relationship. We do the same for the site itself.
  • Associations minimized. We keep any association with a reviewed business, brand or sponsor to a minimum so that no revenue stream can influence a review, and we disclose any that remain.
  • All revenue sources disclosed. Every way the site earns money is listed on how the site is funded.
  • No pre-publication previews. Businesses never see a review before it goes live, and cannot change an opinion or a score.

How scoring works

Each review rates four criteria from 1 to 10. The criteria depend on the category:

CategoryCriteria
ProductsPerformance, Value, Durability, Ease of use
TravelExperience, Value, Getting around, Crowds and authenticity
RestaurantsFood, Service, Atmosphere, Value
WineAroma, Palate, Finish, Value

The overall score is a considered judgment, not a mechanical average, and the review explains it.

Wine quick notes: older bottles from our CellarTracker history appear as short quick notes with a score and, where one was written, a tasting note. CellarTracker uses a 100-point scale, so a 90 there is shown here as 9.0 out of 10. Quick notes have no criteria breakdown, and each one says how the bottle was acquired, or that we no longer have a record.

Scale: 9–10 exceptional · 7–8 good · 5–6 mixed · 3–4 poor · 1–2 avoid.

Testing standards

  • Products: used for a meaningful period before publishing, with the duration stated and at least one alternative compared where possible.
  • Travel: visited in person, with the dates and season stated, costs given, and any discount, comp or hosting disclosed.
  • Wine: tasted with the bottle's vintage, producer and price noted, with how long it was open and how it changed where we tracked that.
  • Restaurants: visited at least twice where practical, ordering like any customer, without announcing that we write reviews.

Every review has the same parts

Bottom line, who it is for, how we tested or visited, scores, what worked, flaws but not dealbreakers, and what we would skip. Using one format makes reviews comparable and makes it harder to hide a weak spot.

Updates and corrections

Reviews are dated and updated when things change. Errors are corrected in the open: see corrections.
