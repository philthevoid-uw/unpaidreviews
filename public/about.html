Unpaid Reviews is a community that publishes independent reviews of products, travel, restaurants and wine, written by screened reviewers who are real people and are not employees of, or funded by, what they review. Every review says how the item was obtained. We do not accept payments that would influence a review, we are not paid for affiliate links, and no revenue incentive influences what is reviewed or how it is scored. Any advertising will be clearly visible, and every review must follow the same policy. See how we review and how the site is funded.
