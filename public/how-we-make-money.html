How the site is funded

Status as of November 24, 2026: no ads are running, no affiliate links exist, and the site earns no money. Any change to this will be announced here before it takes effect.

What will never change

  • No affiliate links or commissions on anything I review.
  • No payment of any kind from a business, brand or person I review, or from their competitors, in exchange for coverage, placement, ranking or silence.
  • No sponsored content, gifts, free stays, free meals or free products.
  • No link between the money the site earns and which products, places or restaurants are reviewed, or how they are scored.

If advertising is ever added

I am considering whether to run advertising. I have not decided. If I do, these rules will apply, and I will publish the specific details on this page before any ad runs:

  • Automated, with no deals. Ads are bought and priced automatically through an ad marketplace. There is no sales team, no negotiated pricing and no special terms for anyone.
  • No favoritism and no penalties. Neither pricing nor ad placement depends on what I have reviewed, what I plan to review, or how anything was scored. Advertisers get no advance look at reviews and no influence over them.
  • Same rules for every advertiser. I will not hand-pick advertisers. Any restrictions will be broad categories applied site-wide (for example, no gambling ads) and listed publicly.
  • Clearly labeled and separate. Ads will be marked as ads and kept out of review text.
  • Reviewed businesses. I will publish the exact rule for ads from businesses I have reviewed before ads start.

Other ways the site could be supported

Reader support (donations or memberships) is one option I may explore. Any model I use must pass one test: no revenue is tied to the products, places or restaurants I review.
