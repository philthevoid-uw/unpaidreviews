How the site is funded

Status as of November 24, 2026: no ads are running, no affiliate links exist, and the site earns no money. Any change to this will be announced here before it takes effect.

What will never change

  • We do not accept payments that would influence the content of a review, from any business, brand or person we review, or from their competitors, in exchange for coverage, placement, ranking or silence.
  • We are not paid for affiliate links, commissions, referral fees or per-sale payments, and we do not try to influence the site or the purchase decisions people make in any other way.
  • No revenue incentive influences what is reviewed or not reviewed, or whether anything is reviewed positively or negatively.
  • Every review is written by our community of screened reviewers, who are real people and not employees of, or funded by, what they review. All reviews follow this same policy, and the community is built on these guiding principles.
  • Every revenue source is disclosed on this page, and any association that could create a revenue stream is kept to a minimum.

If we run advertising or promotions

We have not decided whether to run advertising or other revenue-generating promotions. If we do, these rules apply, and we will publish the specific details on this page before any ad or promotion runs:

  • Clearly visible. Ads and paid promotions are labeled as such and kept separate from review text.
  • Not influenced by advertisers. Who does or does not advertise has no effect on what we review, what we skip, or how anything is scored. Advertisers get no advance look at reviews and no influence over them.
  • No favoritism and no penalties. Neither pricing nor ad placement depends on what we have reviewed, what we plan to review, or how anything was scored.
  • Same rules for every advertiser. Ads are sold on the same terms to everyone, with no negotiated deals. Any restrictions are broad categories applied site-wide (for example, no gambling ads) and listed publicly.
  • Reviewed businesses. We will publish the exact rule for ads from businesses we have reviewed before ads start.

Other ways the site could be supported

Reader support (donations or memberships) is one option we may explore. Any model we use must pass one test: nothing is paid based on purchases of what we review, and no revenue incentive influences what is reviewed or how it is scored. Every source of revenue will be disclosed on this page.
