Reader reviews: the plan

Draft. Nothing here is live yet.

For now, every review is written by one person. The plan is to open the site to reader reviews once the foundation is solid. These are the principles I intend to hold to:

  • Reviews from real experience. Readers will say how they used the product or visited the place.
  • No incentives. Nobody can offer, request or reward a review.
  • Businesses cannot edit or buy their way out. They may respond publicly to a review, and the review stays.
  • Transparent moderation. Published rules, and a regular report on what was removed and why.
  • Reader and editor ratings stay separate. My scores will never be blended into the community score.
