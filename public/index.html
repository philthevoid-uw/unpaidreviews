How we protect independence

Written by screened reviewers

Reviews are written by our community of reviewers. Every reviewer is screened to confirm they are a real person, not a bot, and not an employee of, or otherwise funded by, what they review.

No payments that shape reviews

We do not accept payments that could influence what a review says. How each item was obtained is disclosed on every review, including anything that was provided or discounted.

Nothing steers what you buy

We are not paid for affiliate links, and we do not try to influence the site or your purchase decisions in other ways. No revenue incentive decides what gets reviewed, what is skipped, or whether a review is positive or negative. Reviews from our community follow the same policy.

Advertising is visible

Any advertising or paid promotion will be clearly labeled, and we will not be influenced by who does or does not advertise here. How the site is funded.

Mistakes are public

Errors are fixed in the open and logged on the corrections page. Scores are never changed because someone complained.

You help pick what's next

Tell us what to buy, visit or eat. Suggestions from the businesses involved are ignored.
