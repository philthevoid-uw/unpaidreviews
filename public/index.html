How I protect independence

I pay for everything

Products are bought at retail. Meals and trips are paid in full, with no reviewer discounts and no tourism-board hosting.

Nothing is for sale

Neither this site nor its owners are paid when you buy something I review. No revenue incentive decides what gets reviewed, what is skipped, or whether a review is positive or negative. Reader reviews follow the same policy.

Mistakes are public

Errors are fixed in the open and logged on the corrections page. Scores are never changed because someone complained.

You pick what's next

Tell me what to buy, visit or eat. Suggestions from the businesses involved are ignored.
