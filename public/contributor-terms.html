What happens to uploads

Uploads are held privately until a person approves them. We re-encode images, which removes hidden metadata such as location, and we may refuse, edit for formatting, or remove any content at any time, including on a rights or safety complaint. Report a problem at hello@unpaidreviews.com.

Accounts

We may suspend an account that breaks these terms or the reviewer standards. You can ask us to delete your account at any time.
